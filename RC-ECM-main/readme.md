# RC24 Energy Control Module (ECM)

This is the firmware for the Energy Control Module, which does the following things:

 - Measurement of voltages on the 12 V, 24 V, 36 V and supercapacitor busbars
 - Reporting of VESC current and voltage
 - Calculation and reporting of instantaneous energy efficiency
 - Calculation and reporting of super capacitor energy
 - Reporting of contactor status
 - Reporting of DC-DC health status
